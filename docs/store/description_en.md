## Description

The slime flood is coming – every week! Turn your bunker into a fortress, pull up walls, raise towers and stop the horde.

**Build your base**
Sawmill, quarry, scrap press, refinery: gather resources, upgrade buildings through ten levels and build walls of wood, stone and metal. Villagers work in your town, carry wood and repair walls.

**Defend against the weekly flood**
Once a week the great flood rolls in: flyers, brutes, bosses. Ten tower types – from the machine gun to the Tesla coil – and your hero with special skills decide between victory and defeat.

**Explore the world**
Eight areas from Green Vale to Volcano Isle: drive out in your vehicle, collect loot, free outposts and defeat mighty bosses.

**Heroes, weapons and companions**
Six heroes with 15 weapons each, talents and gear. Twelve companions carry loot, fight alongside you or sniff out treasure.

**Stronger together**
Find friends by their bunker name, send gifts, help with building, join a clan and climb the leaderboards.

**Fair play**
Loot crates, weapons and resources can't be bought with money – strength is earned by playing. Gems only speed up waiting times and unlock looks. No ads.

Rustwall can also be played offline; your progress is synced as soon as you're back online.
