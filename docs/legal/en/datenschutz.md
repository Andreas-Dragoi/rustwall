## 2. Summary

- Rustwall also works entirely without a server. In that case all data stays on your device.
- If online play is switched on, we create an account without a name and without an email address. It is only linked to a random ID that the game generates itself (no device number).
- We send game data for improving the game (telemetry) and error reports **only with your consent**. For players under 16 we don't send them at all.
- We show no ads, sell no data and don't follow you across other apps or websites (no "tracking").
- You can delete your account in the game at any time: Settings → Online and privacy → Delete account.

## 3. What data do we process?

### 3.1 Playing without a server
Saves, settings and bunker names are stored only on your device (in the browser: in the website's storage). We receive none of it.

### 3.2 Online account and cloud storage
If a server address is set or online play is active, we process:

- **Account ID**: a random device ID (e.g. "rw-3f9c…"), an account number and login keys (session tokens).
- **Save** of each city (buildings, resources, heroes, progress), with version number and save time, so you can keep playing on another device.
- **Public player data**: bunker name, in-game player name, friend code, player level, leaderboard points, clan. Other players can see these.
- **Social data**: friends list, mail (gifts, help), clan membership and clan messages, reports and block list.
- **Gem balance and rewards**, so they can be confirmed fairly by the server.
- **Optionally linked login**: email address and password (stored encrypted) or the ID from "Sign in with Apple" or Google – only if you link it yourself to take your save to a new device.
- **Technical connection data**: IP address and time of the request in the server logs (at most {{14}} days).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract – you want to play online), for the logs Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (secure operation, protection against abuse).

### 3.3 Purchases
If you buy gems, payment is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We receive no payment data, only a receipt (product, transaction number, time), which our server checks with Apple or Google before the gems are credited. We keep receipts for up to {{10}} years due to statutory retention obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR); after an account deletion without any link to your account.

### 3.4 Game data for improvement (telemetry) – only with consent
With your consent we send anonymous events: start and length of a play session, tutorial steps, result of a flood or expedition, gems earned and spent, frame rate, device model, operating system, language, screen size. The server stores these events without an account number (only with a daily changing, non-reversible ID).
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent), § 25(1) TDDDG. Retention: at most {{13}} months.

### 3.5 Error reports – only with consent
With your consent, the game sends a report when a program error occurs: error message, location in the program, device model, operating system, game version. No saves, no names. The recipient is our server or the service {{SENTRY OR "NONE" – if Sentry: Functional Software, Inc., USA, data processing agreement, EU data region}}.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Retention: at most {{90}} days.

### 3.6 What we don't do
No ads, no tracking across other providers' apps or websites, no passing on to data brokers, no location, no access to contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

## 5. Children

Rustwall is meant for all ages. On first start the game asks whether you are under 16. If so, no telemetry and no error reports are sent, and the shop clearly points out parental permission before every purchase. There is no free text chat between strangers outside of clans; clan messages are filtered and can be reported.
